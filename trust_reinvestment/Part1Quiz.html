Part 1 game rules
1. Player 1 starts with 10 points. Player 2 receives no initial endowment.
2. Player 1 chooses a whole number from 0 to 10 to send.
3. Player 2 receives three times the amount Player 1 sent.
4. Player 2 chooses a whole-number amount to return to Player 1, from 0 up to the amount received.
Player 1 payoff = 10 − amount sent + amount returned
Player 2 payoff = three times the amount sent − amount returned
