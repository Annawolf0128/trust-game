{{ block title }}Part 2 Rules{{ endblock }} {{ block content }}

Your pair remains the same. The same rules apply in every Part 2 round.

In each round, player 1 chooses how many points to send to you. The amount player 1 sends is multiplied by {{ C.MULTIPLIER }}, and you receive the full multiplied amount. You then choose how many points to return to player 1. The computer applies a random adjustment to the amount you return before it reaches player 1:

  • 60% chance: amount that reaches player 1 = amount you return
  • 20% chance: amount that reaches player 1 = amount you return x 0
  • 20% chance: amount that reaches player 1 = amount you return x 2

The computer adjustment affects only player 1's earnings. Your earnings always equal the amount you receive minus the amount you choose to return.

player 1 earnings = points player 1 keeps from the endowment + amount that reaches player 1

your earnings = amount you receive - points you return to player 1

At the end of each round, your earnings from that round are added to your Part 2 accumulated account.

Player 1 sees only the amount that reaches them after the computer adjustment, not the amount you chose to return.

Part 2 lasts for at least {{ min_rounds }} rounds. The total number of rounds is determined by the computer and will not be announced in advance.

After the round is completed, you and player 1 will be informed whether Part 2 will continue.
