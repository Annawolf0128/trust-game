Your pair remains the same. The same rules apply in every Part 2 round.

In each round, you receive an endowment of {{ C.ENDOWMENT }}. You may send points to player 2 from this endowment and from your Part 2 accumulated account.

amount you send = points from the current-round endowment + points from your Part 2 accumulated account

The amount you send is multiplied by {{ C.MULTIPLIER }}, and player 2 receives the full multiplied amount. Player 2 then chooses how many points to return to you. The computer applies a random adjustment to the amount player 2 returns before it reaches you:

  • 60% chance: amount that reaches you = amount player 2 returns
  • 20% chance: amount that reaches you = amount player 2 returns x 0
  • 20% chance: amount that reaches you = amount player 2 returns x 2

The computer adjustment affects only your earnings. It does not change player 2's earnings.

your earnings = points kept from the current-round endowment + amount that reaches you from player 2

player 2 earnings = amount player 2 receives - points player 2 returns

At the end of each round, your earnings from that round are added to your Part 2 accumulated account, and any points you sent from that account are subtracted from it.

You see only the amount that reaches you after the computer adjustment, not the amount player 2 chose to return.

Part 2 lasts for at least {{ min_rounds }} rounds. The total number of rounds is determined by the computer and will not be announced in advance.

After the round is completed, you and player 2 will be informed whether Part 2 will continue.
