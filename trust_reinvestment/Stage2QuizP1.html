Part 2 game rules
You remain Player 1 and remain matched with the same participant throughout Part 2.
Part 2 lasts for at least 5 rounds. After Round 5, each completed round is followed by an 80% chance of another round and a 20% chance that Part 2 ends.
At the start of every round, Player 1 receives a 10-point endowment. Player 2 receives no endowment.
Player 2 receives three times the amount Player 1 sent.
Each round payoff is added to the accumulated Part 2 account. Your Part 2 payoff equals the final balance in that account.
